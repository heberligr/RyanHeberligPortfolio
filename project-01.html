Raceway Power Distribution System

Replaced failure-prone, free-hanging wiring on a low-pressure molding station with a protected raceway — taking downtime to zero.

Role
Manufacturing Engineering Co-Op
Organization
Amphenol PCD
Tools
Creo · Part Selection · Machining · Wiring
Date
Spring 2026
1.0The problem

Station failures stopped production.

16 : 8Molds to stations

The line ran 16 molds on 8 stations, and failing heating cartridges and thermocouples kept taking stations offline. Cartridges had to be modified before use and stuck in the molds, and the free-hanging wiring was hard to reach.

A bad mold could sit out a run. A bad station cut concurrent run time.

2.0The approach

Protect the station, standardize the parts.

I rebuilt the station's power distribution around a raceway that lifts wiring away from the heated molds and keeps it easy to reach. I worked with suppliers on stainless-braided cartridges that drop in with no modification, and upgraded to larger thermocouples, voltage-matched connectors, and coolant manifolds in place of leaky PVC.

I 3D printed the brackets, machined parts, and wired and assembled the raceway myself, with input from the operators.

3.0The result

Zero downtime.

+25%Concurrent molding capacity

Station-side failures were designed out, and nearly all remaining failures are quick thermocouple swaps.

The correctly specced connectors draw less power, so more molds can run at once.
