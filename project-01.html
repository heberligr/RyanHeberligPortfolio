Raceway Power Distribution System

Replaced failure-prone, free-hanging wiring on a low-pressure molding station with a protected raceway — taking downtime to zero.

01 · Problem

Station failures stopped production

The line ran 16 molds on 8 stations, and hardware failures kept taking stations offline.

Stuck cartridges

Heating cartridges needed modifying before use and stuck in the molds, so every failure meant downtime.

Unreachable wiring

Free-hanging station wiring was hard to access and replace.

Stations are the bottleneck

A bad mold can sit out a run. A bad station cuts concurrent run time.

02 · Approach

Protect the station, standardize the parts

I rebuilt the station's power distribution around a raceway and replaced failure-prone parts with durable, standard ones.

Raceway

Lifts wiring away from the heated molds and keeps it easy to reach for maintenance.

Standard cartridges

Stainless-braided cartridges, sourced with suppliers, that drop in with no modification.

Better parts

Larger thermocouples, voltage-matched connectors, and coolant manifolds in place of leaky PVC.

Hands-on build

I 3D printed brackets, machined parts, and wired the raceway myself, with input from operators.

03 · Result

Zero downtime

Station-side failures were designed out, and nearly all remaining failures are quick thermocouple swaps.

+25% capacity

The correctly specced connectors draw less power, so more molds can run at once.
