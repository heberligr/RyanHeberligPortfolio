Raceway Power Distribution System

Replaced failure-prone, free-hanging wiring on a low-pressure molding station with a protected raceway — taking downtime to zero.

Problem

Failures stopped stations

  • Heating cartridges and thermocouples kept failing
  • Free-hanging station wiring was hard to reach
  • 16 molds, 8 stations: a bad station cuts run time
Approach

Protect the station, standardize parts

  • Elevated, protected raceway wiring
  • Ready-to-swap braided heating cartridges
  • Bigger thermocouples, correctly specced connectors
Result

Zero downtime

  • Station-side failures designed out
  • Remaining failures are quick swaps
  • +25% capacity from lower power draw
01

What I did

  • Raceway layoutLifted wiring away from the heated molds to stop heat damage and fraying, and made it easy to reach for maintenance.
  • Heating cartridgesWorked with suppliers on standard, stainless-braided cartridges that need no modification — swap in immediately.
  • ThermocouplesUpgraded to larger, more robust units that are less prone to damage.
  • ConnectorsReplaced incorrectly specced plugs with voltage-matched connectors that draw less power.
  • CoolantSwapped the leaky PVC coolant lines for manifolds.
  • Build3D printed brackets, machined parts, and wired and assembled the raceway myself, with operator input on daily use.
