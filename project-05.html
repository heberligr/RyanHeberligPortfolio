F-01

Pneumatic diode lead-bending fixture

3 min → 20 s · −89%
Problem
  • Multistep, mostly manual: ~3 min per part
  • Leads bent to the wrong shape
  • Pressed into housings by hand with pliers
Design
  • Rotary bending stage + pneumatic press in one fixture
  • Leads bent to match the part, then pressed straight in
Result
  • ~3 min → ~20 s (−89%)
  • Correct lead geometry before pressing
F-02

Universal ultrasonic-welding clamp system

1 plate · 3 swappable fixture sets
Problem
  • Every welder fixture had its own clamp and base plate
  • Extra cost, hardware, and storage space
Design
  • One universal clamping plate
  • 3 fixture sets that mount to it
Result
  • Swappable fixturing, far less duplicate hardware
  • Cheaper, faster replacements for broken fixtures
F-03

Multi-contact diode press head

5 contacts at once · −15% process time
Problem
  • Old head pressed one contact at a time
  • Play in the arbor forced a pause to realign
Design
  • Locates on the part itself, not the arbor
  • Presses all 5 contacts at once
Result
  • No realigning; −15% total process time
  • CAD on file for replacement tooling
