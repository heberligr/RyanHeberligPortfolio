F-01

Pneumatic diode lead-bending fixture

3 min → 20 s · −89%

Turned a slow, mostly manual bend-and-press into one fast operation.

Problem

Leads were bent to the wrong shape, then pressed into housings by hand with pliers: about 3 minutes per part.

Design

A rotary bending stage and a pneumatic press in one fixture bend the leads to match the part, then press them straight in.

Result

About 20 seconds per part, an 89% reduction.

F-02

Universal ultrasonic-welding clamp system

1 plate · 3 fixture sets

One shared plate replaced a dedicated clamp and base for every welder fixture.

Problem

Each ultrasonic-welder fixture had its own clamp and bottom plate, adding cost, hardware, and storage.

Design

A universal clamping plate with three fixture sets that mount to it.

Result

Swappable fixturing with far less duplicate hardware, and cheaper replacements for broken fixtures.

F-03

Multi-contact diode press head

5 contacts · −15% time

Pressing all five contacts at once removed the stop-and-realign step.

Problem

The old head pressed one contact at a time, and play in the arbor forced the operator to pause and realign.

Design

A new head that locates on the part itself, not the arbor, and presses all 5 contacts together.

Result

15% less total process time, with CAD on file for replacement tooling.
