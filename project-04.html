DIY Nerf Gun

A custom spring-piston foam dart blaster, designed from scratch in CAD and prototyped piece by piece.

01 · Challenge

Seal without binding

The plunger has to build enough pressure to fire reliably, without leaking air or binding as it cycles.

How it works

Pulling the plunger compresses a spring. Releasing it pushes a burst of air that fires the dart.

02 · Approach

Model, print, hand-test

Everything is designed from scratch in SolidWorks, with no kits or existing blaster internals.

Iterating on fit

Printing and refining the plunger tube, o-ring seal, and spring mount for a tight, low-friction fit.

03 · Status

Where it stands

The air chamber and plunger are printed and test-fit. The slide and trigger are next.

Done

Air chamber and plunger, cycled by hand.

Next

Slide and trigger system, then mounting everything in the housing.

Housing

Pistol-style CAD is ready to print and may change as the internals develop.
