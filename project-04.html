DIY Nerf Gun

A custom spring-piston foam dart blaster, designed from scratch in CAD and prototyped piece by piece.

Role
Solo Project
Status
● In progress
Tools
SolidWorks · FDM 3D Printing
Date
2026 – Ongoing
1.0The challenge

Seal without binding.

A spring-piston blaster fires by compressing a spring, then releasing it to push a burst of air through the barrel.

The plunger has to build enough pressure to fire reliably, without leaking air or binding as it cycles.

2.0The approach

Model, print, hand-test.

Everything is designed from scratch in SolidWorks, with no kits or existing blaster internals.

I've been printing and refining the plunger tube, o-ring seal, and spring mount for a tight, low-friction fit.

3.0Status

Where it stands.

The air chamber and plunger are printed and test-fit, cycled by hand. Next up is the slide and trigger system.

CAD for the pistol-style housing is ready to print, but may change once the internals are mounted.
