Challenge

Seal without binding

  • Build enough pressure to fire reliably

  • Full blaster modeled in SolidWorks

01

Key details

  • How it worksPulling the plunger compresses a spring; releasing it pushes a burst of air that fires the dart.
  • From scratchNo kits or existing blaster internals — grip frame and air system are all custom.
  • IteratingRefining the plunger tube, o-ring seal, and spring mount for a tight, low-friction fit that won't crack or leak.
  • DoneAir chamber and plunger printed and test-fit, cycled by hand.
  • NextSlide and trigger system, then mounting the mechanisms in the housing.
  • HousingPistol-style housing CAD is ready to print; may change as the internals develop.
