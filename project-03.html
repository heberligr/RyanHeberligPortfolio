Punching Strength Tester

A punching-bag rig that measures punch strength and shows it in real time.

Role
Co-Developer
Course
Cornerstone of Engineering I
Tools
SolidWorks · 3D Printing · C++ · Arduino
Date
Oct – Dec 2024
1.0The problem

Survive a full-force punch.

450+ NRated impact load

For Cornerstone of Engineering I, our team set out to build a punching strength tester that takes repeated full-force punches without the frame or sensor failing.

2.0The approach

Laser-cut structure, real sensing.

I designed and laser cut the target, mounting braces, and electronics housing in SolidWorks, built to survive repeated impacts.

On the electronics side, I turned accelerometer data into a punch-force score in C++ on an Arduino.

3.0The result

Instant feedback.

Press start, throw a punch, and your strength shows up instantly on the LED strip and LCD, with audio feedback too.
