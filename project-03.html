Punching Strength Tester

A punching-bag rig that measures punch strength and shows it in real time.

Role
Co-Developer
Course
Cornerstone of Engineering I
Tools

Date

450+ N

Real-time

C++

Accelerometer scoring on Arduino

01 · Problem

Survive a full-force punch

Build a target and mount that take repeated 450+ N punches without the frame or sensor failing.

02 · Approach

Laser-cut structure + sensing

I designed the structure in SolidWorks and turned an accelerometer signal into a score.

Structure

Laser-cut target, mounting braces, and electronics housing, built for repeated impacts.

Scoring

Accelerometer data converted into a punch-force score in C++ on an Arduino.

03 · Result

Instant feedback

Press start, throw a punch, and see your strength on the LED strip and LCD.

Built to last

Rated for repeated loads over 450 N.
