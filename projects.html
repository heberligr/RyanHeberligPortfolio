Project 01

Raceway Power Distribution System

0 downtime · +25% capacity
  • Creo
  • Part Selection
  • Manual Machining
  • Wiring & Assembly

Redesigned the power distribution system for a low-pressure molding station at Amphenol PCD, replacing failure-prone free-hanging wiring with an organized raceway-style layout. I sourced and integrated custom heating cartridges, upgraded thermocouples, and correctly-specced connectors alongside 3D-printed brackets and hand-wired assembly, isolating failures to quick-swap parts, eliminating downtime and raising concurrent molding capacity by 25%.

Project 02

Tactility — Braille E-Reader

6-pin Braille actuator
  • Onshape
  • 3D Printing
  • Mechanism Design
  • Rapid Prototyping

Built as part of a six-person hardware team at Forge Product Lab, Tactility is a refreshable Braille e-reader that translates PDF text into physical Braille. I designed the actuation mechanism that raises and lowers each Braille cell's pins, fabricated working prototypes with FDM and SLA 3D printing, and integrated them with N20 motors and drive electronics.
