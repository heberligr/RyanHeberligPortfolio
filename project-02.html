Tactility — Refreshable Braille E-Reader

A low-cost device that turns PDF text into refreshable Braille, one line at a time.

01 · Problem

Too small, too expensive

Refreshable Braille displays drive every dot with its own actuator, which is too tiny and too costly for a $350, one-semester build.

The goal

Take a PDF, translate it to Braille, and render it one line at a time.

02 · Approach

A lock-and-key mechanism

Instead of squeezing an actuator behind every dot, I moved the size constraint into the gaps between letters.

Like a door lock

A key bumps pins to different heights based on its shape.

Two motors per cell

N20 DC-motor “keys”, one per column, set the pins for any letter, number, or symbol.

True to standard

Every dot stays at the 1.5 mm Braille size.

03 · Result

A working prototype

The device translates real PDF text into physical Braille, a capability that normally costs thousands.

My role

Designed the mechanism, ran ME system meetings, and led design reviews with our EE Chief.
