Tactility — Refreshable Braille E-Reader

A low-cost device that turns PDF text into refreshable Braille, one line at a time.

Role
Hardware Team Member
Team
Forge Product Lab · 6 people
Tools
Onshape · FDM & SLA · Arduino
Date
Feb – Apr 2026
1.0The problem

Too small, too expensive.

$350Total budget

Refreshable Braille displays drive every dot with its own actuator, which is too tiny and too costly for a one-semester student build.

Our goal was to take a PDF, translate it to Braille, and render it one line at a time.

2.0The approach

A lock-and-key mechanism.

2N20 motors per cell

Instead of squeezing an actuator behind every dot, I moved the size constraint into the gaps between letters, so every dot stays at the true 1.5 mm Braille standard.

It works like a door lock: two N20 motor-driven “keys”, one per column, bump six pins to the right heights for any letter, number, or symbol.

3.0The result

A working prototype.

6Person hardware team

The device translates real PDF text into physical Braille, something that normally costs thousands.

Beyond the mechanism, I ran our ME system meetings and led design reviews with our EE Chief on Forge, a student product-design team.
